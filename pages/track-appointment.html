Important Information

Appointment Status Meanings:

  • Pending: Your appointment request has been received and is awaiting confirmation from our staff.
  • Confirmed: Your appointment has been confirmed. Please arrive 15 minutes early.
  • Cancelled: Your appointment has been cancelled. Contact us to reschedule.

Before Your Appointment:

  • Arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled time
  • Bring a valid ID and insurance card (if applicable)
  • Prepare a list of current medications
  • Contact us at least 24 hours in advance if you need to reschedule
