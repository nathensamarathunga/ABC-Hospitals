Our Story

ABC Hospital has been serving the people of Sri Lanka for over 35 years, providing world-class healthcare services with compassion and excellence. Founded in 1985, we have grown from a small clinic to one of Colombo's most trusted healthcare institutions.

Our commitment to medical excellence, patient care, and technological advancement has made us a leader in Sri Lankan healthcare. We combine traditional values of care and compassion with cutting-edge medical technology to provide the best possible outcomes for our patients.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality healthcare services that improve the health and well-being of our community while maintaining the highest standards of medical ethics and patient safety.

Our Vision

To be Sri Lanka's premier healthcare institution, recognized for excellence in patient care, medical innovation, and community service.
